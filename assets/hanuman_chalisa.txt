# Hanuman Chalisa — Transliteration with Meaning

*Each verse is followed by its meaning in English.*

---

**Shri Guru Charan Saroj Raj**
**Nij mane mukure sudhar**
**Varnao Raghuvar Vimal Jasu**
**Jo dayaku phal char**

> After cleansing the mirror of my mind with the pollen dust of holy Guru's Lotus feet, I profess the pure, untainted glory of Shri Raghuvar (Shri Ram) which bestows the four-fold fruits of life (Dharma, Artha, Kama and Moksha).

---

**Budhi Hin Tanu Janike**
**Sumirau Pavan Kumar**
**Bal budhi Vidya dehu mohe**
**Harahu Kalesa Vikar**

> Fully aware of the deficiency of my intelligence, I concentrate my attention on Pavan Kumar (Hanuman) and humbly ask for strength, intelligence and true knowledge to relieve me of all blemishes causing pain.

---

**Jai Hanuman gyan gun sagar**
**Jai Kapis tihun lok ujagar**

> Victory to thee, O Hanuman! Ocean of Wisdom. All hail to you, O Kapisa — fountain-head of power, wisdom and Shiva-Shakti — you illuminate all the three worlds (the entire cosmos) with your glory.

---

**Ram doot atulit bal dhama**
**Anjani-putra Pavan sut nama**

> You are the divine messenger of Shri Ram, the repository of immeasurable strength, known also as the Son of Pavan (Wind), born of Anjani.

---

**Mahavir Vikram Bajrangi**
**Kumati nivar sumati Ke sangi**

> With limbs as sturdy as Vajra (the mace of God Indra), you are valiant and brave. Good sense and wisdom attend upon you. You dispel the darkness of evil thoughts.

---

**Kanchan varan viraj subesa**
**Kanan Kundal Kunchit Kesa**

> Your physique is beautiful, golden-coloured, and your dress is pretty. You wear earrings and have long, curly hair.

---

**Hath Vajra Aur Dhuvaje Viraje**
**Kandhe moonj janehu sajai**

> You carry in your hand a lightning bolt along with a victory (kesari) flag, and wear the sacred thread upon your shoulder.

---

**Sankar suvan kesri Nandan**
**Tej pratap maha jag vandan**

> As a descendant of Lord Shankar, you are the comfort and pride of Shri Kesari. With the lustre of your vast sway, you are venerated all over the universe.

---

**Vidyavan guni ati chatur**
**Ram kaj karibe ko aatur**

> You are the repository of learning, virtuous and fully accomplished, always keen to carry out the behests of Shri Ram.

---

**Prabu charitra sunibe ko rasiya**
**Ram Lakhan Sita man Basiya**

> You are an ardent listener, always keen to hear the narration of Shri Ram's life stories. Your heart is filled with what Shri Ram stood for — you therefore always dwell in the hearts of Shri Ram, Lakshman, and Sita.

---

**Sukshma roop dhari Siyahi dikhava**
**Vikat roop dhari lanka jarava**

> You appeared before Sita in a diminutive form and spoke to her in humility. You assumed an awesome form and struck terror by setting Lanka on fire.

---

**Bhima roop dhari asur sanghare**
**Ramachandra ke kaj sanvare**

> With overwhelming might you destroyed the Asuras (demons) and performed all tasks assigned to you by Shri Ram with great skill.

---

**Laye Sanjivan Lakhan Jiyaye**
**Shri Raghuvir Harashi ur laye**

> You brought Sanjivan (a herb that revives life) and restored Lakshman back to life. Shri Raghuvir (Shri Ram) cheerfully embraced you with a heart full of joy.

---

**Raghupati Kinhi bahut badai**
**Tum mam priye Bharat-hi sam bhai**

> Shri Raghupati (Shri Ram) lustily extolled your excellence and said: "You are as dear to me as my own brother Bharat."

---

**Sahas badan tumharo yash gaave**
**Us kahi Shripati kanth lagaave**

> Thousands of beings chant hymns of your glory; saying thus, Shri Ram warmly embraced him (Shri Hanuman).

---

**Sankadik Brahmadi Muneesa**
**Narad Sarad sahit Aheesa**

> Prophets like Sanka, even a sage like Lord Brahma, the great hermit Narad, Goddess Saraswati, and Sheshnag (Aheesa, of immeasurable dimensions) —

---

**Yam Kuber Digpal Jahan te**
**Kavi kovid kahi sake kahan te**

> Even Yamraj (God of Death), Kuber (God of Wealth), and the Digpals (guardians of the four corners of the universe) have vied with one another in offering homage to your glories. How then can a mere poet give adequate expression to your supreme excellence?

---

**Tum upkar Sugreevahin keenha**
**Ram milaye rajpad deenha**

> You rendered a great service to Sugriv — you united him with Shri Ram and he was installed on the royal throne.

---

**Tumharo mantra Vibheeshan mana**
**Lankeshwar Bhaye Sub jag jana**

> By heeding your advice, Vibhishan became Lord of Lanka — this is known all over the universe.

---

**Yug sahastra jojan par Bhanu**
**Leelyo tahi madhur phal janu**

> On your own you dashed upon the sun, at a fabulous distance of thousands of miles, mistaking it for a sweet, luscious fruit.

---

**Prabhu mudrika meli mukh mahee**
**Jaladhi langhi gaye achraj nahee**

> Carrying the Lord's signet ring in your mouth, it is hardly any wonder that you easily leapt across the ocean.

---

**Durgaam kaj jagat ke jete**
**Sugam anugraha tumhre tete**

> The burden of all difficult tasks in the world becomes light with your kind grace.

---

**Ram dware tum rakhvare**
**Hoat na agya binu paisare**

> You are the sentry at the door of Shri Ram's divine abode. No one may enter without your permission.

---

**Sub sukh lahai tumhari sarna**
**Tum rakshak kahu ko dar na**

> All comforts of the world lie at your feet — devotees enjoy all divine pleasures and feel fearless under your benign protection.

---

**Aapan tej samharo aapai**
**Teenhon lok hank te kanpai**

> You alone are capable of containing your own splendid valour — all three worlds tremble at your thunderous call.

---

**Bhoot pisach Nikat nahin aavai**
**Mahavir jab naam sunavai**

> All ghosts, demons, and evil forces keep away at the mere mention of your great name, O Mahaveer!

---

**Nase rog harai sab peera**
**Japat nirantar Hanumant beera**

> All diseases, pain, and suffering disappear upon reciting the holy name of Shri Hanuman regularly.

---

**Sankat se Hanuman chudavai**
**Man Karam Vachan dyan jo lavai**

> Those who remember Shri Hanuman in thought, word, and deed with sincerity and faith are rescued from all crises in life.

---

**Sub par Ram tapasvee raja**
**Tin ke kaj sakal Tum saja**

> For all who worship and have faith in Shri Ram, the supreme lord and king of penance, you make all their difficult tasks easy.

---

**Aur manorath jo koi lavai**
**Sohi amit jeevan phal pavai**

> Whoever comes to you for the fulfilment of any desire, with faith and sincerity, secures the imperishable fruit of human life.

---

**Charon Yug partap tumhara**
**Hai persidh jagat ujiyara**

> Through all four ages your magnificent glory is acclaimed far and wide — your fame radiantly illuminates the cosmos.

---

**Sadhu Sant ke tum Rakhware**
**Asur nikandan Ram dulhare**

> You are the saviour and guardian angel of saints and sages, and the destroyer of demons — the angelic darling of Shri Ram.

---

**Ashta sidhi nav nidhi ke dhata**
**Us var deen Janki mata**

> You can grant anyone the eight yogic Siddhis (powers, such as becoming light or heavy at will) and the nine Nidhis (riches, comfort, power, prestige, fame, sweet relationships, etc.) — this boon was conferred upon you by Mother Janki (Sita).

---

**Ram rasayan tumhare pasa**
**Sada raho Raghupati ke dasa**

> You possess the elixir of devotion to Shri Ram — in all rebirths you remain Shri Raghupati's most dedicated disciple.

---

**Tumhare bhajan Ram ko pavai**
**Janam janam ke dukh bisravai**

> Through hymns sung in devotion to you, one can find Shri Ram and become free from the sufferings of many births.

---

**Anth kaal Raghuvir pur jayee**
**Jahan janam Hari-Bakht Kahayee**

> If, at the time of death, one enters the divine abode of Shri Ram, thereafter in all future births he is born as the Lord's devotee.

---

**Aur Devta Chit na dharehi**
**Hanumanth se hi sarve sukh karehi**

> One need not turn to any other deity for propitiation, as devotion to Shri Hanuman alone can bestow all happiness.

---

**Sankat kate mite sab peera**
**Jo sumirai Hanumat Balbeera**

> One is freed from all sufferings and ill-fated contingencies of rebirth in the world — for those who remember and adore the mighty Hanuman.

---

**Jai Jai Jai Hanuman Gosahin**
**Kripa Karahu Gurudev ki nyahin**

> Hail, hail, hail, Shri Hanuman, Lord of the senses! Let your victory over evil be firm and final. Bless me in your capacity as my supreme Guru.

---

**Jo sat bar path kare kohi**
**Chutehi bandhi maha sukh hohi**

> One who recites this Chalisa one hundred times becomes free from the bondage of life and death and finally enjoys supreme bliss.

---

**Jo yah padhe Hanuman Chalisa**
**Hoye siddhi sakhi Gaureesa**

> All those who regularly recite the Hanuman Chalisa (the forty verses) are surely blessed — such is the testimony of none other than Bhagwan Shankar himself.

---

**Tulsidas sada hari chera**
**Keejai Das Hrdaye mein dera**

> Tulsidas, as a bonded servant of the Divine Master, remains perpetually at his feet, praying: "Oh Lord! Enshrine yourself within my heart and soul."

---

**Pavantnai sankat haran,**
**Mangal murti roop.**

> Oh conqueror of the Wind, destroyer of all miseries, you are the very symbol of auspiciousness.

**Ram Lakhan Sita sahit,**
**Hrdaye basahu sur bhoop.**

> Along with Shri Ram, Lakshman, and Sita, reside in my heart, O King of the Gods.

---

*🙏 Jai Hanuman 🙏*
